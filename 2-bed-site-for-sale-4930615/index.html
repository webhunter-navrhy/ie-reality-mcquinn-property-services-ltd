Description

McQuinn Property Services is delighted to present this traditional two-bedroom cottage, set on approximately 0.5 acres in a truly spectacular coastal setting. Nestled just off the iconic Wild Atlantic Way, this property offers breath-taking panoramic views across to the Dingle Peninsula, making it an ideal opportunity to create a dream coastal retreat.

This home is expected to qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant, offering potential buyers an excellent incentive to restore and personalize this unique property to its full potential.

With a gross internal floor area of c. 64 sq m (669 sq ft), the property provides a cozy footprint with endless possibilities for enhancement. Its elevated position, combined with uninterrupted sea views and direct road frontage, ensures that this property stands out as a hidden gem on Kerry Head.

This property is ideal for anyone seeking a project for either a holiday home, a peaceful retreat, or permanent coastal residence. With views that truly speak for themselves, it must be seen in person to be fully appreciated.
